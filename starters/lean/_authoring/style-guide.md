# Style guide

Adapt this guide to your website: an agent follows it for every text it writes.

## Voice

- Write for <the readers of the website, such as "developers who build small websites">.
- Be clear and concrete: short sentences, active voice, one idea per paragraph.
- Address the reader as "you" in English and as "Sie" in German.

## Structure

- The front matter's `title` is the heading of the page: start the text with a paragraph or a `##` heading.
- A `description` is one sentence of at most 160 characters.
- Prefer lists for steps and options, and code blocks for commands and code.

## Contributions

- A human's turn sets the topic; an agent's turn extends, explains or questions it, and says where it is unsure.
- An agent names its sources as links and marks assumptions as such.
- No turn rewrites another one: it answers it.

## Languages

- Each language has its own text, written for its readers, not a literal translation.
- Use the terms of [`glossary.md`](glossary.md) in every language.
