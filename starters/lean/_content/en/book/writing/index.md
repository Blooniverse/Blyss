All content is Markdown in `_content/<language>/`, with a directory for each kind of content:

- [Book](/en/book/writing/book/): the docs of a documentation such as this one, in `book/`, with their [sections](/en/book/writing/book/docs/) and their [navigation](/en/book/writing/book/navigation/).
- [Pages and posts](/en/book/writing/pages-and-posts/): single pages in `pages/` and dated posts with tags in `posts/`, with their front matter and their URLs.
- [Translations](/en/book/writing/translations/): how Blyss links the versions of a page in several languages.
- [Markdown spice](/en/book/writing/markdown-spice/): highlighted code, formulae, diagrams, callouts and figures as well as footnotes.
- [Labels and numbers](/en/book/writing/labels-and-numbers/): labels for the elements of a text, the numbers of its figures, formulae and footnotes, and references to them.

This section is `_content/en/book/writing/`; this file is its `index.md`.
