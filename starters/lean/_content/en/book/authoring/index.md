All content is Markdown in `_content/<language>/`, with a directory for each kind of content:

- [Pages and posts](/en/book/authoring/pages-and-posts/): single pages in `pages/` and dated posts with tags in `posts/`, with their front matter and their URLs.
- [Books](/en/book/authoring/books/): the docs of a documentation such as this one, in `book/` and in the directory of each further book, with their [sections](/en/book/authoring/books/docs/) and their [navigation](/en/book/authoring/books/navigation/).
- [Translations](/en/book/authoring/translations/): how Blyss links the versions of a page in several languages.
- [Markdown spice](/en/book/authoring/markdown-spice/): formulae, diagrams, callouts and figures as well as highlighted code and footnotes.
- [Agentic co-authoring](/en/book/authoring/agentic-co-authoring/): the turns of a text that a human and an AI agent write together, and the workflow behind them.
- [Labels and numbers](/en/book/authoring/labels-numbers-references/): labels for the elements of a text, the numbers of its figures, formulae and footnotes, and references to them.

This section is `_content/en/book/authoring/`; this file is its `index.md`.
